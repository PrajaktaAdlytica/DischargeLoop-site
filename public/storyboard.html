DischargeLoop / Safe Harbor / Storyboard 1.0

Mira’s discharge.
Four handoffs. A human decision.

The first connected sequence introduces one synthetic episode, exposes what remains unresolved, assigns visible owners, and stops at human review. All dates, records and sources are fictional. This is an isolated, read-only demonstration.

Discharge → Four unresolved handoffs → Assigned owners → Human review
00 / Discharge

A clear next step.

Background
Warm paper #F5F1E7. Harbor ink copy and ample reading space.
Route
Measured branches and circular nodes share the same coordinate system, without stretched dots or separating outlines. Thin teal branches draw from Mira’s discharge point to four handoff nodes over approximately 1.4 seconds with a short stagger. Orange begins only at the unresolved review branch. Each node appears as its route arrives.
Content
Mira Vale, DL-001, Monday 5 October 2026 at 14:00. PCP appointment pending; pharmacy pickup unconfirmed; contact attempt unanswered; medication-list difference awaiting pharmacist review.
Interaction
Follow a discharge scrolls to chapter 01. Selecting a hero handoff scrolls to its selected detail. Skip to review bypasses intermediate chapters.
01 / The unfinished handoff

Make the four questions visible.

Background
As this chapter approaches, paper moves through sea-glass #DDEAE4 into harbor teal #174D50. The change begins during the discharge chapter and follows native scroll progress; foreground colours are selected for readable contrast.
Route
A thin orange continuation at the chapter exit draws toward the next chapter. It connects the narrative without crossing text or implying task completion.
Evidence
Selecting any of the four handoffs reveals a specific explanation with source access. Unknown pharmacy pickup stays unknown; a requested appointment is not treated as confirmed.
Reverse scroll
Background and route retrace predictably. Entrance reveals remain visible. Selected item stays selected.
02 / Ownership

A point of responsibility.

Background
The scroll transition settles into harbor teal #174D50 before the ownership chapter; warm paper text maintains contrast.
Product scene
The owner matrix remains in a sticky desktop workbench while the narrative scrolls. All four tasks show their named fictional review role, status and next review time. Selecting a matrix row updates the linked source.
Accountability
“Assigned doesn’t mean resolved.” The episode remains at four open items. No automatic green completion.
Mobile
Pinning is removed. Ownership copy, review copy, and workbench stack in normal reading order. Direct chapter navigation remains available.
03 / Human review

The evidence appears. The decision stays human.

Background
Harbor teal deepens to harbor ink #102D3A as the review section comes into view.
Evidence reveal
A source strip expands below the matrix with the selected document ID. View source opens a native modal containing the fictional source excerpt, responsible reviewer, dated next review, and open-state explanation. Escape or Close returns to the story.
Typed draft
On entering the review chapter, the 108-character draft types at 24ms per character, approximately 2.6 seconds. It is permanently labelled UNSENT — REQUIRES REVIEW. It is never sent. The full text is available to assistive technology without announcing every typed character.
Replay
Replay draft deliberately repeats the typing. Scroll changes do not continuously retype a completed draft. Returning to the chapter shows the completed draft.
Endpoint
The orange terminal remains open with “Awaiting a human decision.” Medication differences remain for professional review. No clinical, coding or billing determination occurs.

Motion and accessibility

  • Native scroll, visible chapter buttons, a skip link, readable text, keyboard focus and touch controls.
  • Motion toggle switches to complete still states. Operating-system reduced-motion preference is respected automatically.
  • Mobile removes the pinned scene. All text, evidence and the full draft remain accessible.
  • Source dialogs are keyboard-contained with native dialog behaviour; Escape closes them.
  • Audit CTA opens a preview inquiry only. Nothing is transmitted or stored; the completion state explicitly says no request was sent.

This is the first story prototype, not the full multi-page website. No external services, patient data, live EHR, outbound messages or real appointments are connected.
